  For All Users

Quick Reference Card

Step-by-step checklists for the most common tasks on the IPAWAS platform. Print this page and keep it at your desk for quick reference during training or day-to-day use.

A. Tasks for All Users

How to Log In Both

1
Go to the IPAWAS website

Open your browser and navigate to the IPAWAS platform URL.

2
Click "Login" in the top navigation
3
Enter your email and password → Click "Sign In"

You will be sent to your dashboard automatically.

Forgot your password?

Click "Forgot password?" on the login page. A reset link will be emailed to you. It expires in 24 hours.

How to Accept an Invitation & Create Your Account Both

1
Check your email inbox

Look for "You've been invited to join IPAWAS". Check Spam if you don't see it.

2
Click "Accept Invitation" in the email

Do not share this link — it is unique to you.

3
Fill in your name and create a password → Click "Create My Account"

You will be automatically logged in and taken to your dashboard.

How to Change Your Password Both

1
In the sidebar, click "My Settings" → "Password"
2
Enter your current password, then your new password twice
3
Click "Save Password"

B. Tasks for IPA Staff

How to Create an Investment Opportunity IPA Staff

1
Sidebar → Opportunities → "Create Opportunity"
2
Fill in the title and description

Write clearly for international investors. Avoid local jargon.

3
Select the opportunity type, primary sector, and location
4
Enter the investment range (min and max in USD)
5
Fill in ROI, payback period, market analysis, and risk factors
6
Upload a thumbnail image

Required before publishing. Min recommended size: 800×600px.

7
Click "Save as Draft"

Review it once more before publishing.

Before saving, review:

Title is specific and clear • All amounts are in USD • A thumbnail image is uploaded • There are no spelling errors

How to Publish an Opportunity IPA Staff

1
Sidebar → Opportunities

Find the opportunity with "Draft" status.

2
Click "Publish"

A confirmation message appears.

3
Confirm — the opportunity is now live

Status changes to "Published". Investors can now see it on the public website.

No Publish button?

You need the "Can Publish" permission. Contact your team admin or IPAWAS HQ.

How to Handle an Investor Inquiry IPA Staff

1
Sidebar → Inquiries

New inquiries have a red "New" badge.

2
Click the inquiry to open it

Read the investor's full message and note their contact email.

3
Change status to "In Progress"

This tells your team it is being handled.

4
Email the investor directly from your work email

The platform does not send replies — you contact them yourself.

5
Update status as the conversation progresses

"Qualified" when they are serious. "Closed" when concluded.

Respond within 48 hours

Delays send a negative signal to investors. Check your inquiries every morning.

How to Add a Priority Sector IPA Staff

1
Sidebar → Sectors → "Add Sector"
2
Select the sector from the dropdown
3
Write a 2–3 sentence description

Explain why this sector is a priority for your country.

4
Set investment potential (High / Medium / Low) → Click Save

How to Update Your Country Profile IPA Staff

1
Sidebar → Profile → Overview

Check the completion percentage and the checklist to see what's missing.

2
Click the section you need to update

Basic Information, Economic Data, Media Gallery, SEO Settings, or Resources.

3
Make your changes and click "Save Changes"

How to Invite a New Team Member IPA Staff

1
Sidebar → Invitations → "Send Invitation"
2
Enter their email address and select their role
3
Optionally add a personal welcome message
4
Click "Send Invitation"

They receive an email with a link valid for 7 days. If it expires, use "Resend".

C. Tasks for HQ Admins

How to Invite a User to a Member State HQ Admin

1
Sidebar → Member States → click the country
2
Click "Invite User"
3
Enter email, select role → Click "Send Invitation"

To invite up to 50 people at once: Invitations → Bulk Invite

How to Deactivate a User HQ Admin

1
Sidebar → All Users → click the user's name
2
Click "Deactivate User" → Confirm

The user is immediately locked out. Their history is preserved. You can reactivate at any time.

How to Change a User's Permissions HQ Admin

1
All Users → click the user → "Manage Permissions"
2
Toggle permissions on or off

Can Edit Profile • Can Create Opportunities • Can Publish • Can Manage Users • Can View Analytics

3
Click "Save Permissions"

Changes apply immediately.

How to Export Platform Data HQ Admin

1
Sidebar → Reports → "Export Data"
2
Select the dataset

Member States, Opportunities, or Platform Stats.

3
Click "Export" — the CSV file downloads to your computer

Open in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets.

E. Common Issues & Solutions

ProblemLikely CauseSolution
I can't log in Wrong password or email Use "Forgot password?" on the login page. If still stuck, contact your admin.
My invitation link doesn't work Link expired (over 7 days old) Ask the person who invited you to resend the invitation.
I don't see the "Publish" button Missing "Can Publish" permission Contact your team admin or IPAWAS HQ to have this permission granted.
I don't see the "Create Opportunity" button Missing "Can Create Opportunities" permission Contact your team admin or IPAWAS HQ.
My profile completion is stuck below 100% A required item is missing Go to Profile → Overview and check the checklist. The missing items are marked.
I uploaded an image but it looks blurry Image resolution too low Use a higher resolution image. Hero image minimum: 1200×600px.
A team member can't log in after I invited them They may not have accepted yet, or link expired Check Invitations list. Resend if status shows "Expired".
I accidentally published something Clicked Publish instead of Draft Click "Unpublish" on the opportunity — it returns to Draft status immediately.
The page shows a 500 error Server-side issue Refresh the page. If it persists, contact IPAWAS IT support.
I can't find a page I was on before Navigation confusion Use the sidebar — every section is accessible from there. Or start from /dashboard/.

F. Glossary of Terms

TermDefinition
DraftContent saved but not yet visible to the public or investors.
PublishedContent that is live and visible on the public IPAWAS website.
FeaturedA published item highlighted prominently at the top of its listing page.
InquiryA message from an investor expressing interest in investing in a country or specific opportunity.
InvitationAn email sent to a new user to join the platform. Contains a unique, time-limited link.
Member StateA country that is a member of IPAWAS and has its own IPA registered on the platform.
IPAInvestment Promotion Agency — the government body responsible for attracting foreign investment into a country.
HQ AdminAn administrator at IPAWAS Headquarters with full access to all member states and system settings.
IPA StaffA staff member of a country's IPA with access only to their own country's dashboard.
Profile CompletionA percentage score showing how much of a country's profile has been filled in. 100% is the target.
OpportunityA specific investment project or sector opportunity posted by an IPA to attract investors.
ROIReturn on Investment — the expected financial return an investor will earn from their investment.
SEOSearch Engine Optimisation — settings that help your pages appear higher in Google search results.
Activity LogA record of every action performed on the platform — who did what and when.
PermissionA specific right granted to a user account that allows them to perform a certain action.
DeactivateTo block a user account without deleting it. The user cannot log in; their history is preserved.
Bulk InvitationInviting multiple users (up to 50) at once using a single action.
