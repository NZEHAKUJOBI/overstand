Getting Started

This section walks you through your very first experience with the IPAWAS platform — from receiving your invitation email to exploring your dashboard. Even if you have never used a web-based management tool before, this guide will get you up and running confidently.

Step 1 — Accepting Your Invitation

You do not create your own account on IPAWAS. An administrator will send you a personalised invitation email. Here's what to expect:

1
Check your email inbox

Look for an email from the IPAWAS platform with the subject line "You've been invited to join IPAWAS". Check your Spam/Junk folder if you don't see it within a few minutes.

2
Click the invitation link

Inside the email, there is a blue "Accept Invitation" button. Click it. This link is unique to you and expires after 7 days — do not share it with anyone.

3
Create your password

You will be taken to a registration page. Enter your first name, last name, and choose a secure password. Your email address is already filled in automatically.

4
Click "Create My Account"

You will be automatically logged in and taken directly to your dashboard — no need to log in separately.

Invitation link expired?

If the link has expired (older than 7 days), contact the person who invited you and ask them to resend the invitation. Do not try to register without an invitation link.

Password tips

Use at least 8 characters, mixing upper and lower case letters, numbers, and a symbol (e.g. IpaWest@2026). Avoid using your name or "password123".

Step 2 — Logging In

After your first login, you will need to log in each time you return to the platform.

1
Go to the IPAWAS platform

Open your web browser (Chrome, Firefox, or Edge recommended) and navigate to the IPAWAS website. Click "Login" in the top navigation bar.

2
Enter your email and password

Type the email address that was invited and the password you set during registration.

3
Click "Sign In"

The system will check your credentials and automatically send you to the correct dashboard based on your role.

3
Click the link in the email and set a new password

Step 4 — Setting Up Your Profile

Before you start using the platform, take a moment to complete your personal profile.

1
Click your name in the sidebar (bottom left)

This opens the account settings section.

2
Go to "My Settings" or "Profile"

Here you can update your first name, last name, and contact details.

3
Change your password (recommended on first login)

Navigate to Settings → Password and set a new, strong password that only you know.

Change your password on first login

Although the invitation process already has you set a password, it is good practice to change it again after your first session to ensure it is private.

Step 5 — Logging Out Safely

Always log out when you are done, especially if you are using a shared or public computer.

1
Find "Logout" in the sidebar

Scroll to the bottom of the left sidebar. You will see a Logout link.

2
Click "Logout"

You will be immediately signed out and redirected to the home page. Your session is ended securely.

Never close the browser tab without logging out

Simply closing the tab may leave your session active, allowing someone else using the same computer to access your account. Always click Logout first.

Getting Started — Summary

Before you begin using the platform, confirm you have done all of the following:

  • ✅  Accepted your invitation email and created your account
  • ✅  Logged in successfully with your email and password
  • ✅  Familiarised yourself with the dashboard layout (sidebar, header, content area)
  • ✅  Updated your personal profile with your correct name
  • ✅  Changed your password to something secure and private
  • ✅  Know how to log out when you're done
