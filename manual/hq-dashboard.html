HQ Admin Dashboard Guide

The HQ Admin dashboard is the command centre for the entire IPAWAS platform. As an HQ Admin, you have a bird's-eye view of every member state, every user, all published content, and all investor inquiries across West Africa. This guide explains every section in detail.

HQ Admin access only

The pages described in this section are only visible to users with the HQ Admin role. IPA Staff will not see these menus.

1. Dashboard Overview

When you log in as an HQ Admin, you land on the Overview page at /dashboard/hq/. This is your main control panel. It gives you an instant snapshot of the entire platform.

The Four Summary Cards

At the top of the overview page are four cards that give you key platform numbers at a glance:

Member States

Total registered member states and how many are currently active.

Opportunities

Total investment opportunities on the platform, with a % change vs. the previous month.

Inquiries

Total investor inquiries received, with how many are still pending a response.

Platform Users

Total IPA staff accounts and how many were active in the last 7 days.

Top Performing Member States

A ranked table showing the top 5 member states by number of investment opportunities. For each country, you can see:

  • Rank — Position 1 to 5 based on total opportunities
  • Opportunities — Total count published
  • Inquiries — Total investor inquiries received
  • Team Size — Number of active staff in that IPA
  • Status — Whether their profile is Active (published) or Draft

System Health Indicators

Three progress bars showing overall platform utilisation:

  • Platform Utilisation — How many member states have at least one opportunity posted
  • Published Profiles — How many member states have active/published profiles
  • Avg Opportunities per State — The average number of opportunities per member state

Recent Platform Activities

The last 15 actions taken by any user across any member state — a live feed of what's happening on the platform. Each entry shows who did what, which country they belong to, and when.

2. Managing Member States

The Member States section allows you to see and manage every IPA registered on the platform. Access it via Sidebar → Member States.

The Member States List

Fig. 6

Member States list page
Shows a searchable, filterable table of all member states with columns for Name, Country, Opportunities (total/published), Inquiries, Team Size.

You can:

  • Search — Type a country name or acronym in the search box
  • Filter by status — Show only Active or Draft member states
  • Sort — Click column headers to sort by name, opportunity count, or inquiry count
  • Click a row — Opens the detailed view for that member state

Member State Detail View

Clicking any member state opens its detailed view. This shows:

  • Profile Completion — A percentage showing how complete the country's profile is
  • Team members — List of all IPA staff for that country (name, role, active status)
  • Opportunities — Count of total, published, and draft opportunities
  • Inquiries — Count of all investor inquiries received
  • Recent activities — Last 10 actions taken by that member state's staff
  • Pending invitations — Invites sent but not yet accepted

Inviting a User to a Member State

To add a new staff member to a specific country's IPA:

1
Open the Member State detail page

Navigate to Member States → click the country name.

2
Click "Invite User"

A form will appear asking for the new user's email address and role.

3
Fill in the details and click "Send Invitation"

The invitee will receive an email with a link to create their account.

Need to invite multiple people at once?

Use the Bulk Invitations feature under Sidebar → Invitations → Bulk Invite. You can invite up to 50 people in a single action.

Member State Analytics

Each member state has its own analytics page (Member States → [Country] → Analytics). It shows:

  • Opportunities trend over the last 30 days
  • Inquiries by month
  • Opportunity breakdown by sector
  • Inquiry conversion rate (how many inquiries turned into qualified leads)

3. Managing Users

Go to Sidebar → All Users to see every IPA staff member across all member states on the platform.

Fig. 8

System Users list page
Shows a searchable table of all IPA staff with columns: Name, Email, Member State, Role, Last Login, Status, Activity Count.

Searching and Filtering Users

  • Search by email address, first name, or last name
  • Filter by member state — show users from a specific country only
  • Filter by role — Admins, Editors, Viewers, etc.
  • Filter by status — Active or Inactive users

Viewing a User's Details

Click any user's name to open their detail page. You will see:

  • Full name, email, member state, and role
  • Account status (Active / Inactive)
  • Date joined, last login, and total login count
  • Number of actions in the last 30 days
  • Their 20 most recent activities on the platform

Editing a User

1
Open the user's detail page

Users list → click the user's name.

2
Click "Edit User"

A form opens where you can update their first name, last name, or email address.

3
Make your changes and click "Save"

The changes take effect immediately.

Changing a User's Permissions

Each IPA staff account has granular permissions that control what they can do within their country's dashboard. To change them:

1
Open the user's detail page → click "Manage Permissions"
2
Toggle the permissions on or off

Available permissions:

  • Can Edit Profile — Update the country's public profile
  • Can Create Opportunities — Add new investment opportunities
  • Can Publish — Make content live on the public site
  • Can Manage Users — Invite and manage team members
  • Can View Analytics — Access charts and statistics
3
Click "Save Permissions"

Changes apply immediately — the user will see the updated menus on their next page load.

Be careful with "Can Publish"

Users with the Publish permission can make investment opportunities and content live on the public-facing website immediately. Only grant this to trusted, senior staff.

Deactivating a User

If a staff member leaves or should no longer have access, deactivate their account (do not delete it, as their activity history is preserved).

1
Open the user's detail page
2
Click "Deactivate User"

A confirmation prompt will appear.

3
Confirm deactivation

The user is immediately locked out and cannot log in. You can reactivate them at any time from the same page.

Sending a Single Invitation

1
Click "Send Invitation"

Top-right button on the Invitations page.

2
Fill in the form

Required: email address, role (e.g. Editor, Admin), and member state.
Optional: a personalised invitation message shown in the email.

3
Click "Send Invitation"

The invitation email is dispatched immediately. You will see a confirmation on screen.

Bulk Invitations (up to 50 at once)

1
Click "Bulk Invite" on the Invitations page
2
Enter multiple email addresses

One per line or separated by commas. Duplicates are automatically removed.

3
Select the member state and role for all invitees

All invites in one bulk action must go to the same country and role.

4
Click "Send All Invitations"

You'll see a detailed report: how many succeeded, any that failed, and why.

5. Reports & Analytics

The platform has two separate reporting tools — Reports (summary tables and exports) and Analytics (charts and trend graphs). Both are in the sidebar.

Reports Overview

Navigate to Sidebar → Reports. Three reports are available:

Platform Statistics

Single-page summary of total member states, opportunities, inquiries, and users — with 30-day comparisons.

Member State Comparison

Side-by-side table ranking all member states by opportunities, inquiries, and team size.

Activity Summary

What types of actions are being performed most, and daily activity trends over the last 30 days.

Exporting Data to Excel/CSV

From the Reports page, click "Export Data". You can export three datasets:

  • Member States — Name, acronym, opportunities, inquiries, team size, status
  • Opportunities — Title, member state, sector, investment range, status, date created
  • Platform Stats — Key metrics snapshot

Files download as .csv which can be opened in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets.

Platform Analytics

Navigate to Sidebar → Analytics. This section has four views:

Analytics PageWhat You'll See
Overview Summary of all platform metrics — member states, opportunities, inquiries — with a 90-day activity trend chart and 6-month opportunity creation trend.
Opportunities Breakdown by status (published/draft), opportunity type, sector, member state. Monthly creation trend for the past 12 months. Investment tier distribution.
Inquiries Breakdown by status (new/in progress/qualified), top member states by inquiry volume, monthly trend, daily trend, conversion rate.
Traffic / Activity Daily activity trend, most common action types, most active member states, and the most active individual users — all over the past 30 days.

Filtering Logs

  • Action Type — e.g. "Opportunity Published", "User Invited", "Login", "Profile Updated"
  • User — Search by name or email to see what a specific person has done
  • Date Range — Narrow down to a specific period
Use logs for accountability

If content was changed or deleted unexpectedly, check the logs to see who made the change and when. This is your most reliable tool for investigating platform activity.

7. Knowledge Hub — Publishing News

The Knowledge Hub allows you to publish platform-wide news articles that are visible to all IPA staff. Access it via Sidebar → News.

The News List

Shows all HQ news articles with their title, status (Published / Draft), category, and date. You can search by title or filter by category and status.

Creating a News Article

1
Click "Create Article"

Top-right button on the News list page.

2
Fill in the article details

Required fields: Title, Excerpt (a short summary), Category, and Publication Date.
Optional: Author name, Author title, Featured Image, Gallery images.

3
Write the article body

Use the rich text editor to format text, add headings, bullet points, and links.

4
Add optional metadata

Tags, related topics, related countries, related sectors — these help users find the article.

5
Choose to Publish or Save as Draft

Draft — saves the article but it is not visible to others yet.
Publish — makes it live immediately.
Featured — tick this box to highlight the article at the top of the news section.

Auto-save is enabled

While you are writing, the system automatically saves your draft every few minutes. You will see a "Saved" indicator. If your browser closes unexpectedly, your work should be preserved.

8. System Settings

Navigate to Sidebar → System Settings. This section contains advanced platform configuration — use with care.

Setting PageWhat It ControlsWhen to Use
System ConfigOverview of system stats — user count, member states, opportunitiesQuick system health check
Email SettingsEmail server configuration (host, port, backend)If platform emails stop sending
Security SettingsSecurity policies and configurationsPolicy reviews
Maintenance ModePut the platform into maintenance mode (disables public access)During upgrades or outages
Maintenance Mode warning

Enabling Maintenance Mode will make the platform inaccessible to all users, including IPA Staff. Only activate this if you are performing scheduled maintenance and have notified all users in advance.

Personal Settings (HQ Admin)

Under Sidebar → My Settings, you can manage your own account:

  • Settings — Update your personal details
  • Password — Change your login password
  • Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) — Add an extra layer of security to your login (strongly recommended for HQ Admins)
  • Notification Preferences — Choose which platform events you want to be notified about
Enable 2FA for your HQ Admin account

As an HQ Admin, your account has access to every part of the platform. Adding Two-Factor Authentication means that even if your password is stolen, no one can log in without your phone.
