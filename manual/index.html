IPAWAS Platform

Official User Manual & Training Guide

This manual covers everything you need to know to use the IPAWAS platform — from your very first login to publishing investment opportunities and managing your team. Step-by-step instructions written for all experience levels.

Version 1.0 • April 2026 • IPAWAS Secretariat

The IPAWAS Digital Platform is the central hub for all Investment Promotion Agencies (IPAs) across West Africa. It allows each member state to manage and publish their investment profile, opportunities, and respond to investor inquiries — all in one place.

Who Uses This Platform?

There are two types of users on the IPAWAS platform. Your account type determines which dashboard you see and what you can do.

HQ Admin IPAWAS Headquarters

Full system access. Manages all member states, all users, platform-wide reports, analytics, and system settings. Typically 1–3 people at the IPAWAS Secretariat.

IPA Staff Country / Member State

Access limited to their own country's dashboard. Manages opportunities, investor inquiries, country profile, and their team. Each IPA has its own staff.

Not sure which type you are?

When you log in, the platform automatically sends you to your correct dashboard. HQ Admins go to /dashboard/hq/ and IPA Staff go to /dashboard/your-country/.

Getting Help

If you encounter an issue not covered in this manual, here's how to get help:

  • Technical issues (login problems, errors): Contact the IPAWAS IT team
  • Content questions (what to publish, how to write opportunities): Contact your supervisor or the HQ team
  • Password reset: Click "Forgot password?" on the login page
