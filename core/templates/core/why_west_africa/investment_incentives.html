{% translate "Attractive Incentive Packages" %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}West African countries offer competitive incentive packages designed to attract and retain investments across priority sectors. These incentives significantly reduce operational costs and enhance project returns.{% endblocktranslate %}

💰

{% translate "Fiscal Incentives" %}

  • Tax holidays ranging from 3 to 15 years depending on sector and location
  • Reduced corporate income tax rates (as low as 0-15%) for priority investments
  • Customs duty exemptions on imported machinery, equipment, and raw materials
  • VAT exemptions on equipment and specified inputs
  • Investment allowances of 10-100% of capital expenditure
  • Accelerated depreciation for qualifying assets
  • Withholding tax relief on dividends, interest, and royalties
🏢

{% translate "Non-Fiscal Incentives" %}

  • Subsidized land or land at nominal cost in industrial zones
  • Free Trade Zone benefits with full tax exemptions for 10-25 years
  • Ready-built factory spaces available in industrial parks
  • Streamlined permits and one-stop-shop services through IPAs
  • Infrastructure support including utilities, roads, and connectivity
  • Expedited visa and work permit processing
  • Training grants and workforce development programs
🛡️

{% translate "Guarantees & Protections" %}

  • Profit repatriation guaranteed without restrictions
  • Capital repatriation rights fully protected
  • Protection against nationalization and expropriation
  • Bilateral investment treaties providing legal safeguards
  • International arbitration access for dispute resolution
  • Stability clauses protecting against adverse regulatory changes
  • Foreign exchange access guaranteed for eligible transactions
