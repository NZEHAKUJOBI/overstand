{% translate "Decision-Making Process" %}

Transparent and inclusive process for policy development and initiative approval

1

{% translate "Proposal Development" %}

Ideas and proposals are developed by member countries, the Secretariat, or technical committees based on identified needs and opportunities.

2

{% translate "Technical Review" %}

The Technical Committee conducts thorough analysis, impact assessment, and feasibility studies for all major proposals.

3

{% translate "Member Consultation" %}

All member countries are consulted through formal channels to gather input, feedback, and build consensus around proposals.

4

{% translate "Executive Committee Review" %}

The Executive Committee reviews proposals, considers member feedback, and makes recommendations to the General Assembly.

5

{% translate "General Assembly Decision" %}

Final decisions are made by the General Assembly through consensus-building or formal voting procedures as outlined in the statutes.

6

{% translate "Implementation & Monitoring" %}

Approved initiatives are implemented by the Secretariat and relevant committees with regular progress monitoring and reporting.
