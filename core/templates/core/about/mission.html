{% translate "Our Mission & Vision" %}

Driving West Africa's economic transformation through coordinated investment promotion and regional integration

{% translate "Our Mission" %}

To enhance investment promotion and economic cooperation among ECOWAS member states through coordinated strategies, capacity building, and unified regional marketing that attracts and facilitates sustainable foreign direct investment.

We are committed to transforming West Africa into a unified investment destination by leveraging our collective strengths, resources, and market potential to create unprecedented opportunities for both investors and our member countries.

{% translate "Our Vision" %}

To position West Africa as the preferred investment destination globally by leveraging regional integration, abundant resources, and dynamic markets.

We envision a future where West Africa leads global investment attraction through seamless coordination, shared prosperity, and sustainable development that benefits all 400+ million citizens across our 15 member nations.

{% translate "Our Core Values" %}

The fundamental principles that guide everything we do

{% translate "Collaboration" %}

Working together for mutual benefit, sharing knowledge, resources, and opportunities to achieve collective success that surpasses individual efforts.

{% translate "Excellence" %}

Delivering world-class services that exceed expectations, maintain the highest professional standards, and continuously improve our impact.

{% translate "Integrity" %}

Maintaining transparent and ethical practices in all our interactions, building trust through honest communication and accountable actions.

{% translate "Innovation" %}

Embracing new approaches and technologies to solve challenges, improve processes, and create innovative solutions for investment promotion.

{% translate "Sustainability" %}

Promoting responsible investment that creates long-term value for communities, environment, and economies across West Africa.

{% translate "Strategic Objectives" %}

Five key priorities driving our regional transformation agenda

1

{% translate "Coordinate Regional Investment Promotion" %}

Harmonize investment promotion activities across member countries, eliminate duplication, and create synergies that amplify collective impact while respecting individual country strengths and priorities.

2

{% translate "Build Capacity of Member IPAs" %}

Enhance the capabilities of investment promotion agencies through training programs, knowledge sharing, best practice exchanges, and access to cutting-edge tools and methodologies.

3

{% translate "Facilitate Cross-Border Investments" %}

Remove barriers to intra-regional investment flows, promote regional value chains, and create mechanisms that make cross-border investment as seamless as domestic investment.

4

{% translate "Promote Regional Value Chains" %}

Identify and develop integrated value chains that span multiple countries, creating larger markets, economies of scale, and enhanced competitiveness for West African industries.

5

{% translate "Enhance Investor Confidence" %}

Build and maintain high levels of investor confidence through consistent messaging, reliable information, coordinated aftercare services, and demonstrable results in investment facilitation.
