{% translate "Frequently Asked Questions" %}

IPAWAS (Investment Promotion Agencies of West African States) is a regional association of Investment Promotion Agencies from all 12 ECOWAS member states. We facilitate investment promotion, capacity building, and regional cooperation to position West Africa as a premier investment destination. {% translate "Learn more about us" %}

IPAWAS connects investors with investment opportunities across West Africa. You can explore opportunities on our Investment Opportunities page, and we can help connect you with the relevant country IPA for facilitation and support throughout the investment process.

No, IPAWAS does not charge investors for information, facilitation, or connection services. Our mandate is to support and promote investment in West Africa. All investor services are provided free of charge.

Visit our {% translate "Member States page" %} to find detailed information and contact details for all 12 national Investment Promotion Agencies. Each country profile includes the IPA's website, email, and phone contact.

Yes, we welcome visits by appointment. Please email us at infodesk@ipawas.org or call +234 (0) 906 204 0061 to schedule a meeting. Our office is located in Abuja, Nigeria.

Access comprehensive investment data, trends, and analytics through our West Africa Investment Intelligence System (WAIIS). The portal provides real-time investment information, sector analysis, and economic indicators across the region.

We welcome partnerships with organizations committed to advancing investment in West Africa. Visit our {% translate "Partnerships page" %} to learn about partnership opportunities and submit an application.
