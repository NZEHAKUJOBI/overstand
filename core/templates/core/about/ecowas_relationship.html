{% translate "Independent Yet Aligned: Our ECOWAS Partnership" %}

IPAWAS operates as an independent regional body headquartered in Abuja, Nigeria, but aligns closely with ECOWAS objectives on regional integration, investment, and trade facilitation. It complements ECOWAS' efforts by operationalizing investment promotion at the Member State level and serving as a bridge between ECOWAS institutions and national IPAs.

{% translate "Key Collaboration Areas" %}

{% translate "Regional Integration" %}

IPAWAS actively supports the ECOWAS regional integration agenda by promoting investment opportunities that leverage the free movement of capital and services across member states. We align our initiatives with ECOWAS Vision 2050 and work to create awareness about regional integration benefits among investors and IPAs.

{% translate "Investment Promotion" %}

Joint promotion of West Africa as a unified investment destination through coordinated missions to investor markets, regional investment forums, and shared marketing materials. We work together to showcase the region's collective strengths and competitive advantages to global investors.

{% translate "Data & Intelligence" %}

The West Africa Investment Intelligence System (WAIIS) provides critical data and analytics that support ECOWAS policy decisions. We deliver investment trend analysis, evidence-based policy recommendations, and consolidated regional statistics that inform ECOWAS economic planning.

{% translate "Capacity Building" %}

Training IPAs on ECOWAS protocols, investment regulations, and regional opportunities. We facilitate knowledge sharing on how to leverage ECOWAS frameworks and provide technical assistance that aligns with ECOWAS priorities and regional development objectives.

{% translate "ECOWAS Support and Recognition" %}

{% translate "Technical Assistance and Guidance" %}

ECOWAS provides technical expertise and guidance on regional integration matters, trade facilitation, and investment policy frameworks that strengthen IPAWAS's capacity to serve member IPAs effectively.

{% translate "Platform for Regional Coordination" %}

ECOWAS recognizes IPAWAS as the official network of Investment Promotion Agencies and provides platforms for coordination, dialogue, and collaboration on investment-related matters across the region.

{% translate "Recognition as IPA Network" %}

Official recognition by ECOWAS enhances IPAWAS's credibility and authority in representing member IPAs in regional and international forums, strengthening our collective voice in investment matters.

{% translate "Access to ECOWAS Platforms and Events" %}

IPAWAS participates in ECOWAS meetings, summits, and regional events, ensuring investment promotion perspectives are integrated into broader regional economic development strategies and initiatives.
