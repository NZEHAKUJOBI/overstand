{% translate "Membership Eligibility" %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Ensure your organization meets the fundamental requirements for IPAWAS membership before submitting your application.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "ECOWAS Member State" %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Organization must be based in and officially represent one of the 15 ECOWAS member countries with proper government recognition and mandate.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "Official IPA Designation" %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Must be formally designated as the official Investment Promotion Agency or equivalent organization with legal mandate for investment promotion.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "Government Endorsement" %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Requires official endorsement from relevant government ministry or agency confirming authorization to participate in regional initiatives.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "Financial Capacity" %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Sustainable financial capacity to meet membership obligations including annual fees and participation in network initiatives.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "Frequently Asked Questions" %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Find answers to common questions about IPAWAS membership application and requirements.{% endblocktranslate %}

The complete membership application process typically takes 45-60 days from submission to final decision. This includes initial review (15 days), due diligence assessment (20-30 days), and executive committee evaluation (10-15 days). We will keep you informed throughout the process.

Annual membership fees are structured based on country economic classification and organizational capacity. Fees range from $5,000 to $25,000 USD annually. Specific fee structures and payment terms will be provided during the application review process.

Regional and local investment promotion agencies can apply for Associate Membership status. Associate members receive most benefits but have limited voting rights. The primary requirement is government endorsement and demonstration of significant investment promotion activities.

If an application is not approved, we provide detailed feedback on areas needing improvement. Organizations can reapply after addressing the identified issues, typically after a 6-month waiting period. We also offer technical assistance to help organizations meet membership requirements.

Yes, our membership team provides comprehensive support throughout the application process. This includes pre-application consultations, documentation guidance, and technical assistance. Contact our membership coordinator for personalized support.
