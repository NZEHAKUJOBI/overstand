FUniting West Africa for Global Investment

The Investment Promotion Agencies of West African States (IPAWAS) represents a historic collaboration among 12 ECOWAS member nations, established to coordinate regional investment promotion strategies and amplify West Africa's position as a premier global investment destination.

Through strategic coordination, knowledge sharing, and unified policy advocacy, IPAWAS facilitates seamless cross-border investments while maintaining each nation's unique competitive advantages and sectoral specializations.
