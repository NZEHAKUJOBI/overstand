{% translate "About the Commissioner" %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} Dr. Kalilou Sylla serves as the Commissioner for Economic Affairs and Agriculture at the ECOWAS Commission, a critical role that oversees economic development, trade policy, monetary cooperation, agricultural development, and investment promotion across West Africa. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} With direct oversight of IPAWAS and other investment facilitation mechanisms, Commissioner Sylla plays a pivotal role in shaping the regional investment landscape. His mandate includes coordinating policies that enhance West Africa's competitiveness, attract foreign direct investment, and promote intra-regional trade and economic integration. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "Biography" %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} Dr. Kalilou Sylla was appointed Commissioner for Economic Affairs and Agriculture in February 2022, bringing extensive experience in economic policy, agricultural development, and regional integration. A distinguished economist and development specialist, Dr. Sylla has dedicated his career to advancing Africa's economic transformation. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} Prior to his appointment, Commissioner Sylla held senior positions in various African development institutions where he led initiatives in trade facilitation, agricultural value chain development, and private sector engagement. His deep understanding of West Africa's economic dynamics and his pragmatic approach to policy implementation have been instrumental in driving forward ECOWAS's economic agenda. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} Dr. Sylla holds a doctorate in Economics with specialization in development economics and agricultural policy. His academic research has focused on regional economic integration, food security, and sustainable development in Africa. This scholarly background informs his evidence-based approach to policymaking and program design. {% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} Under his leadership, the Economic Affairs and Agriculture directorate has prioritized accelerating the implementation of the ECOWAS Trade Liberalization Scheme, strengthening agricultural productivity and food security, promoting investment in value-added manufacturing, and enhancing the business environment across member states. His work directly impacts IPAWAS's mission to promote West Africa as a premier investment destination. {% endblocktranslate %}
