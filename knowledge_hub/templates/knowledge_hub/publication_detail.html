{% translate "About This Publication" %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}The West Africa Investment Report 2024 provides a comprehensive analysis of foreign direct investment (FDI) trends, sectoral performance, and emerging opportunities across all 15 ECOWAS member states. This flagship publication draws on exclusive data from the West Africa Investment Intelligence System (WAIIS) and features insights from leading investment promotion experts across the region.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}The report examines the investment climate, regulatory frameworks, infrastructure development, and sector-specific opportunities in agriculture, renewable energy, manufacturing, ICT, and infrastructure. It also analyzes the impact of the African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA) on regional investment flows and provides strategic recommendations for investors and policymakers.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}With contributions from all 15 national investment promotion agencies, this report serves as an essential resource for international investors, development partners, and regional institutions seeking to understand and capitalize on West Africa's investment landscape.{% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "Key Findings" %}

{% translate "FDI Growth" %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Regional FDI inflows increased by 23% to $42.5 billion in 2024, driven by energy and manufacturing investments{% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "Renewable Energy" %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Renewable energy sector attracted $8.2 billion in new investments, representing 19% of total FDI{% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "Manufacturing" %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}Manufacturing FDI grew 31% as companies leverage AfCFTA for regional production hubs{% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "Top Investors" %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}China, UAE, and USA remain leading source countries, accounting for 58% of regional FDI{% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "Job Creation" %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}New FDI projects are expected to create 285,000 direct jobs across the region by 2026{% endblocktranslate %}

{% translate "Digital Economy" %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %}ICT and digital services attracted $3.1 billion, growing 45% year-over-year{% endblocktranslate %}
